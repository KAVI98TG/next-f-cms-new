# V0.14.0 — Customer Workspace Provisioning + Demo Access Governance

## Purpose

V0.14.0 turns the identity, organization and membership foundation from V0.13.0 into a deliberate staff-controlled provisioning workflow for real Customer Workspaces and a completely separate lifecycle for public product demonstrations.

This phase follows the approved architecture direction:

- NEXT F Account registration is identity only.
- A Digital client is the commercial/business record, not the tenant.
- A Customer Organization owns tenancy.
- A Customer Workspace must be provisioned deliberately.
- Membership invitation and invitation acceptance are separate from workspace activation.
- Demo access is not a special flag on a real customer workspace.
- Demo data and production customer data must remain isolated.
- Contract-derived website permissions remain unresolved until the Contract Registry resolver exists.

## Real-customer provisioning lifecycle

The staff CMS now models the following sequence explicitly:

1. Digital Client → Customer Organization relationship exists.
2. Staff requests a Customer Workspace.
3. Workspace remains `requested` and grants no access.
4. Staff starts a provisioning review and selects a verified, active NEXT F Account as the primary customer identity for readiness checks.
5. The system evaluates:
   - account verification/state;
   - active Digital client state;
   - active customer-organization state;
   - explicit client → organization relationship;
   - eligible project and/or subscription/service relationship.
6. Staff approves the review only when blockers are resolved.
7. Approval moves the workspace into `provisioning`; it still grants no customer membership.
8. Staff explicitly attaches authoritative Digital projects and services.
9. Staff explicitly attaches managed sites through Site Connection records.
10. Staff activates the workspace only after required project and service scope is attached.
11. Membership invitations may then be issued separately.
12. Customer invitation acceptance remains an account/customer-side operation for the future Customer Workspace authentication flow.

The implementation does not create duplicate project, service or site entities. It stores references to authoritative NEXT F Digital records.

## Provisioning eligibility

A provisioning review is blocked unless the following are true:

- selected NEXT F Account exists;
- account is active;
- account is verified;
- Digital client is active;
- organization exists;
- organization is classified as `customer`;
- organization is active;
- an explicit active Digital Client → Customer Organization relationship exists;
- the client has at least one non-cancelled project or non-cancelled subscription/service relationship.

The review records its selected primary account, reviewer, note and lifecycle timestamps.

## Workspace resource scope

V0.14.0 introduces explicit workspace-scope records for:

- Digital projects;
- Digital services.

Managed sites continue to use the existing Site Connection model because that is already the correct authoritative bridge between `DigitalSite` and Customer Workspace.

A project may be attached only when it belongs to the workspace Digital client and is not cancelled.

A service may be attached only when it can be derived from an eligible Digital project or subscription for that client. A service package existing in the catalog is not enough by itself.

A site may be attached only when it belongs to the same Digital client. Site connection continues to pin Contract Registry version `1.0.0` and `nextf.site.json`; manifest status remains fail-closed until V0.15 contract resolution.

## Workspace activation

Workspace activation now requires:

- workspace status `provisioning`;
- approved provisioning review;
- no current eligibility blockers;
- at least one eligible project attachment;
- at least one eligible service attachment.

A site is not universally required because some Customer Workspaces may support project/service collaboration before a managed site is connected. When a site is relevant, it is attached explicitly rather than inferred.

Activation records `provisionedAt` and closes the provisioning review as `completed`.

Activation does **not** create or accept a membership.

## Membership invitation lifecycle

The V0.13 membership model is extended with:

- invitation expiry timestamp;
- automatic local normalization of overdue pending invitations to `expired`;
- staff resend operation;
- staff manual expiry operation;
- customer/account-side `acceptInvitation()` operation;
- existing suspension and revocation controls.

Invitation acceptance validates:

- the invitation belongs to the authenticating NEXT F Account;
- invitation state is pending;
- invitation is not expired;
- account remains verified and active;
- Customer Workspace remains active.

The internal Staff CMS intentionally does not provide an “accept on behalf of customer” control.

## Demo Access lifecycle

Demo request lifecycle remains independent:

- `submitted`;
- `under_review`;
- `approved`;
- `rejected`;
- `active`;
- `expired`;
- `revoked`;
- `extended`.

Approval does not create an environment automatically.

Staff must separately provision a Demo Environment.

## Demo Environment model

A Demo Environment contains:

- its own environment ID;
- Demo Access request ID;
- isolated environment key;
- demo template version;
- `synthetic_only` data policy;
- environment lifecycle status;
- restrictions;
- expiry timestamp;
- reset count;
- provisioning actor;
- activation/revocation timestamps.

It deliberately contains **no**:

- production Customer Workspace ID;
- Digital client ID;
- production project ID;
- production site ID;
- production integration credential;
- production publishing credential.

Current enforced restrictions are represented explicitly as:

- synthetic data only;
- no production integrations;
- no production publishing;
- no secret access;
- no real customer data.

Demo environments can be activated, reset, extended and revoked independently.

Expired active environments normalize to `expired`, and the associated demo request is also marked expired.

A demo environment cannot be converted into a production Customer Workspace by changing a boolean or lifecycle state. Real-customer provisioning requires the separate production workflow above.

## Provisioning activity

V0.14.0 adds dedicated provisioning activity records for customer-workspace and demo lifecycle actions. This supplements Platform Audit; it does not replace the shared audit system.

Examples include:

- provisioning review started;
- review blocked;
- review approved;
- project attached;
- service attached;
- site attached;
- workspace activated;
- demo environment provisioned;
- demo activated;
- demo reset;
- demo extended;
- demo revoked.

## Staff CMS UX

NEXT F Digital → Website Platform now exposes separate staff views for:

- Customer Workspaces;
- Memberships;
- Demo Requests;
- Demo Environments;
- Provisioning Activity.

The Customer Workspaces view also includes:

- client → organization links;
- provisioning reviews;
- project/service scope;
- managed Site Connections;
- legacy `PortalAccess` migration assessment.

## What remains intentionally unresolved

V0.14.0 does not implement:

- customer authentication frontend;
- production identity adapter;
- email delivery for invitations;
- Customer Workspace frontend;
- Contract Registry resolver;
- real Site Manifest validation;
- canonical website module/permission IDs;
- service-entitlement policy beyond provisioning eligibility;
- Customer Capability Access Policy;
- field-level customer authorization;
- approval-required website content changes;
- production database/API/queue adapters.

Those remain later phases rather than being approximated through local UI state.
