# V0.4.0 - NEXT F Digital Operations

V0.4.0 converts the Digital Core into a practical service-operations system.

## Added
- Recurring subscriptions and renewals
- Project tasks
- Deliverables
- Client approvals
- Managed client sites
- Support tickets
- Automation registry
- Management reports
- Extended dashboard attention model

## Production boundary
All operational concepts are implemented as provider-independent business models using browser-persistent development data. Cloudflare, payment gateways, email delivery and remote monitoring are intentionally not wired yet.

## Next
Gaming Store Core becomes the next complete business vertical.
