# CMS v1.0.31 — Gaming Support Operations + First-Party Tracking

## Support Operations Center

The Gaming Support & Disputes surface is rebuilt as a SaaS-level operator workspace over the existing canonical support namespaces.

- compact health controls for active, SLA-risk, unassigned, waiting-customer and resolved work;
- queue views for Active, Mine, Unassigned, SLA Risk, Waiting Customer, Resolved and All;
- search by case, subject, order, category, product, customer email and assignee;
- status, priority and category filters;
- SLA-aware sorting and human-readable due/breached state;
- assignment controls including Take case / Assign to me / Unassign;
- safe commerce context for order, payment, fulfillment, risk and refund state;
- direct links to Live Operations and Customer 360;
- full conversation timeline with customer/staff/service author identity preserved;
- explicit Reply to customer vs Internal note modes;
- private NEXT F Media evidence upload/download and safe external evidence references;
- wide responsive case drawer and 10px global typography floor compliance.

The CMS Gaming Support snapshot now preserves the canonical customer/staff/service author kind rather than projecting every message as staff.

## First-Party Tracking in Commerce Analytics

CMS Analytics now consumes the extended canonical Gaming event/order stream and adds:

- Tracking Health state (Active / Quiet / Awaiting data);
- page-view, search, filter, offer-selection and support-open counts;
- top storefront page activity;
- campaign/source performance grouped by source, medium and campaign;
- order → paid conversion by acquisition group;
- collected revenue and estimated margin only when the staff principal has the existing finance permission.

Attribution is read from the server-snapshotted canonical order. GTM is not required as the measurement source of truth.

No CMS D1 migration or new CMS secret is required.
