# V0.13.0 — Identity & Membership Foundation

## Purpose

This phase implements the minimum tenancy and authorization data model required before a real Customer Workspace authentication flow can be connected.

It follows the approved architecture rule:

> Account identity, customer organization, Customer Workspace, membership, customer role, service entitlement, managed site capability, and staff authorization are separate concepts.

## Implemented records

### NEXT F Account

Canonical identity record with:

- account ID;
- primary email;
- display name;
- verification state;
- account state;
- optional production-authentication reference placeholder;
- created/updated timestamps.

A NEXT F Account contains **no Platform staff role IDs and no Customer Workspace rights**.

### Staff identity link

A separate record links a NEXT F Account to a Platform staff principal. Staff authorization continues to come from the existing Platform User + Role/RBAC model.

### Customer Organization

Platform organizations now carry an explicit classification:

- `internal`;
- `customer`.

A customer organization is the tenancy owner. It is not a Digital client profile and it is not a person/account.

### Digital Client → Customer Organization link

The commercial Digital client profile is connected to the customer tenancy owner only through an explicit relationship record.

This prevents the system from assuming that creating a client automatically creates a tenant.

### Customer Workspace

V0.12 Customer Workspace records now include both:

- `clientId` — Digital commercial/business relationship;
- `organizationId` — customer tenancy owner.

Existing V0.12 local records without `organizationId` are normalized only when exactly one safe organization relationship can be resolved. Otherwise the organization remains unresolved and staff must repair it explicitly.

### Customer Membership

Membership is now the explicit identity-to-workspace authorization bridge:

- `accountId`;
- `workspaceId`;
- `customerRoleId`;
- membership status;
- invitation state;
- invitation timestamp;
- accepted timestamp;
- revoked timestamp;
- creator/staff principal;
- audit timestamps.

Invitation and active membership are deliberately separate states.

### Customer Roles

Customer roles are separate from Platform staff roles. The initial business-role catalog is:

- Owner;
- Customer Admin;
- Content Editor;
- Marketing User;
- Billing Contact;
- Contributor;
- Read Only.

Their `contractPermissionBindings` arrays are intentionally empty in V0.13.0. No website operation, module permission, field policy, or publish permission is invented before the Contract Registry resolver exists.

## Membership safety checks

A membership invitation can be staged only when:

1. the NEXT F Account exists;
2. the account state is active;
3. the account is verified;
4. the Customer Workspace exists;
5. the Customer Workspace is active;
6. the customer role exists and is active;
7. there is no existing non-revoked membership for the same account/workspace pair.

The staff CMS can suspend or revoke memberships. Customer-side invitation acceptance is not simulated as a staff action; the real acceptance/authentication flow belongs to the separate Customer Workspace and production identity adapter.

## Legacy PortalAccess migration

The old Digital `PortalAccess` model remains preserved for migration evidence.

V0.13.0 adds a read-only migration assessment with these states:

- `blocked_identity`;
- `blocked_organization`;
- `blocked_workspace`;
- `ready_for_review`.

Important safeguards:

- email equality alone never creates a NEXT F Account;
- a legacy `active` status never becomes an active membership automatically;
- a single explicit customer organization must be resolved;
- a single target Customer Workspace must be resolved;
- even `ready_for_review` performs no migration automatically.

Actual controlled conversion can be implemented only through a later reviewed migration/provisioning action.

## Shared Accounts clarification

The pre-existing Platform Shared Accounts view remains available because it provides useful cross-business commerce/customer context. It has been relabeled in the UI as a legacy **shared customer profile aggregation** and is explicitly not the NEXT F Account authentication authority.

## What is intentionally not implemented

- Production customer registration.
- Email verification delivery.
- Password/passkey/OAuth authentication.
- Account recovery.
- Customer Workspace frontend.
- Customer invitation acceptance frontend.
- Service entitlement resolver.
- Contract Registry resolver.
- Site Manifest validation.
- Canonical website permission binding.
- Customer capability access policy.
- Approval-required website changes.
- Production database/API adapters.

These remain future phases rather than being approximated with unsafe local shortcuts.
